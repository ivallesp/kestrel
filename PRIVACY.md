# Kestrel privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. This policy covers the Kestrel Android app (`app.kestrel`), from GitHub and from Google
Play.

**In short:** Kestrel has no server and no account. What you do in Kestrel stays on your phone, and its developer
receives nothing.

## What Kestrel keeps on your phone

- The listings you add, your maximums, your groups and each snipe's timeline.
- Your settings.
- Your eBay session: the cookies that eBay's own sign-in page sets inside Kestrel's browser view. You type your
  password into eBay's page, and it goes to eBay. Kestrel does not store it.

Kestrel turns off Android's cloud backup for this data. Signing out in Settings removes the eBay session from the
phone. Uninstalling Kestrel deletes everything it kept.

## Who Kestrel talks to

- **eBay.** Kestrel loads eBay's pages and photos, as a browser would, to read your listings, check that you are
  signed in, run dry runs and place the bids you set. What eBay receives is covered by eBay's privacy notice.
- **time.google.com.** Kestrel checks the phone's clock against it, so bids leave on the right second. The check sends
  no personal data.

Kestrel uses no analytics, ads, crash reporting or trackers, and it turns off the browser view's usage reports.

## Problem reports

Settings › About › Send a problem report writes a text file on your phone. It lists your recent snipes (titles, item
numbers, timelines) and the app's recent freezes and crashes. Kestrel sends it nowhere: you decide whether to share
it, for example in a message.

## Children

Kestrel is not meant for children. eBay requires its members to be 18 or older.

## Changes
