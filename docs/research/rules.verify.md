## Additional findings
- AF1 robots.txt 'Disallow: /bfl/' covers the bid-placement flow, not only bid history. Paths seen in 2026 third-party captures and code: GET /bfl/placebid/{itemId}?...&module=1, POST /bfl/placebid?action=trisk and POST /bfl/placebid?action=confirmbid&modules=POWER_BID_LAYER&ocv=1 (mvanhorn/printing-press-library, capture folder 2026-04-30, commit 2026-11-03). Bid history is https://www.ebay.co.uk/bfl/viewbids/{id}. Implication: every bid our app places hits a robots-disallowed path, and the UK UA forbids bypassing robot exclusion headers. Keep traffic human-paced and never crawl /bfl/viewbids (inferred).
- AF2 eBay challenge pages, with concrete URLs to detect in the WebView. Wayback shows 307 redirects to https://www.ebay.com/splashui/captcha?ap=1&appName=orch&ru=<urlencoded original>&iid=<uuid>&iim=..&iia=..&iiz=.. (captures 2026-08-09, 2026-10-17, 2026-10-20). It also shows a 302 to https://www.ebay.com/splashui/distil?ap=1&appName=orch&page=block&iid=.. (capture 2026-09-28). adbertram/cli-tools (commit 2026-11-03) documents /splashui/challenge, titled 'Pardon Our Interruption...' with body 'Checking your browser before you access eBay. Your browser will redirect to your requested content shortly.', which clears itself. The same repo documents a page titled 'Error Page' with body 'SORRY Something went wrong on our end <reference id>' as a sticky request-rate wall. Implication: detect these URLs and titles, show them to the user, back off with jitter, never retry in a tight loop, and run a check well before the snipe.
- AF3 The 403 came from eBay's Akamai edge (inferred). Reference 0.566bdc17.1793760840.cb1980e decodes to epoch 1793760840 = 2026-11-04T02:54:00Z (the same second the cookie jar was written). 566bdc17 byte-reversed is 23.220.107.86, inside Akamai's 23.192.0.0/11. A 2026 traffic capture reports 'Akamai Bot Manager active. ak_bmsc, bm_s, bm_ss cookies present' plus a telemetry beacon at an obfuscated path. Implication: the block is an edge decision on this IP, so a phone's mobile or home IP in a real WebView faces different odds; keep request volume low.
