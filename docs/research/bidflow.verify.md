## Additional findings
- MF1 Phone verification can block a snipe. Since July 2025 eBay can require a phone/SMS code to bid on a specific auction. eBay community staff, quoted by VAR (2025-07-12, updated 2025-07-17, /var/tmp/sniper-research/rules/web/var_phone): 'When you complete the verification once you won't need to do it again for the same auction ... you may be prompted to verify on future auctions.' Users report the code arriving after about a minute or never. eBay UK staff gave the purpose as 'reducing sniping, shill bidding and unpaid item situations'. Implication: a T-2 s snipe can hit a verification prompt and fail. The app must detect any verification UI in the bid layer, report it as a distinct failure, and tell the user early (it cannot complete the code in seconds).
- MF2 The bid layer can contain extra steps beyond amount + submit. UK help id=4003 (WebFetch 2026-11-04): 'You may be asked to provide a payment method and postage address when making a bid.' and 'if you win the auction, payment will be taken automatically'. US help (snapshot 2026-10-10): the same, plus 'Starting September 2026, some buyers will begin to see an Autopay at option after you place a bid'. US automatic-bidding help (2026-10-19): 'If you are placing high bids for the first time on eBay, we may ask you to verify your identity or take other standard security steps'. Implication: open the layer early (for example at T-60 s), detect unexpected dialogs, and treat anything other than the amount input + submit as 'needs user'.
- MF3 UK Buyer Protection fee is inside bids. eBay UK help (snapshots 2025-06-11, 2025-11-06, 2026-07-10, 2026-10-25 in /var/tmp/sniper-research/rules/wayback/uk_bpf_* and uk_buyer-protection-fee_20261025014429.html): the fee 'is already included in the item price that you see when browsing on the site and in any bids or offers you make'. The current formula is £0.10 flat + 7% up to £20 + 4% £20-£300 + 2% £300-£4,000. It reproduces the 2026-09 fixture exactly (£50 → £2.70, £90 BIN → £4.30). The 2025-11 and 2026-01 page values (£15.94 incl. £0.94; £27.37 incl. £1.45) fit neither published formula (unexplained). SEMANTIC_DATA.currentBidPrice, BUY_BOX.bidPrice and JSON-LD price all include the fee. Implication: take the user's maximum as fee-inclusive, compare it with the displayed prices, and check that the bid layer's minimum uses the same basis.
- MF4 JSON-LD offers.price is the BIN price, not the current bid, on auction-plus-BIN listings. In dealfinder tests/fixtures/ebay_item_ended_unsold_2026-09.html, JSON-LD price is 94.3 while BUY_BOX.bidPrice is £52.70 and binModel.price is £94.30. Implication: never read the auction price from JSON-LD.
- MF5 STATUS_MESSAGE dates can be one day off. 'Bidding ended on Sun, 16 Nov at 2:10 AM.' is shown for endDate 2025-11-17T02:10:01Z (146949522734, EBAY_US viewed on ebay.co.uk). 'Item sold on Wed, 23 Sep at 7:15 AM.' is shown for biddingEndedAt 2026-09-24T06:15:06Z, while appendText says '24 Sep, 2026 07:15:06 BST' (dealfinder sold fixture, EBAY_DE). The date matches eBay's fixed UTC-7 'MST' date and the time matches the viewer's zone (inferred from 2 samples; UK-local samples ending in daytime agree on both). Implication: never parse the status text for times; use auctionData.biddingEndedAt and listingStatus.
- MF6 'Continue with Google' will not work in the app's WebView. Google OAuth 2.0 Policies (developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2/policies, last modified 2026-08-05): 'A developer must not direct a Google OAuth 2.0 authorization request to an embedded user-agent under the developer's control.' This matches jq6l43d1 README:28 (Google sign-in blocked in automated browsers; set an eBay password). Implication: onboarding must ask the user to sign in with an eBay password (plus any passkey or emailed code), and must detect the Google button path.
